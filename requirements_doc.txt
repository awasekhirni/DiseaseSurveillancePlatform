Disease Monitoring Platform 
Awase Khirni Syed Ph.D.
Comprehensive Feature Matrix 

Last Updated: 2025/Aug/4

The objective of this project to build a global disease monitoring platform, a strategic global initiative aimed at enhancing and protecting citizens worldwide through the development of a decentralized,real-time, interoperable disease surveillance platform. This will enable early detection, rapid response, and data-driven decision-making for infectious and various kinds of disease, thereby improving population health outcomes, reducing economic burden, and strengthening health security.

Modules 




Module 1: Data Integestion Framework 

This module provides secure, standardized ingestion of data from clinical, laboratory, community, wastewater, open-source media, and traditional medicine sources. It supports real-time and batch flows with IPFS storage and blockchain-anchored provenance. Why this module matters:
•	CDC and ProMED emphasize the value of 29+ years of historical data for trend detection and outbreak prediction. 
•	Health map demonstrate that open-source, multi-source ingestion enables early signal detection 
•	without robust ingestion, even the best analytics fail 
•	in decentralized systems, provenance, and trust are as important as data itself.

Module 2: Case and Event Management 
This module provides end-to-end workflow for reporting, investigating, and managing individual cases and outbreaks, with configurable workflows, contact tracing, and blockchain-verified case confirmation. Why this module matters:
•	longitudinal case data enables trend analysis, variant tracking and policy evaluation.
•	It shows real-time alerts that need rapid case triage and escalation
•	without structured case workflows, even perfect data leads to delayed or missed responses. 
•	In decentralized systems, blockchain-verified case confirmation ensures trust across jurisdictions without central authority.

Module 8: Security and Identity 
This module is the trust and integrity layer of the entire platform. In a decentralized, blockchain-anchored system handling sensitive public health data across borders, security, and identity are not just technical concerns, they are foundational to credibility, compliance, and long-term usability. 
This module provide Decentralized Identity (DIDs), zero-knowledge authentication, end-to-end encryption, and immutable audit logs via blockchain. Why this module matters:
•	long-term data integrity is essential, if data is compromised, altered, or lost, historical trend analysis fails. 
•	In decentralized systems, traditional central authentication fails, Decentralized Identifiers (DIDs) and verifiable credentials enable trust without central contorl. 
•	Including traditional healers and community reporters requires privacy -preserving authentication that protects identity while ensuring accountability.
•	Blockchain-anchored audit logs provide immutable proof-of-data provenance, essential for regulatory compliance and scientific reproducibility.
