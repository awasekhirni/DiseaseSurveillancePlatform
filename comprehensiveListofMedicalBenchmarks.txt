Key Benchmark Categories(Legendd):

    Knowledge & Reasoning: Tests factual recall and clinical deduction (e.g., MedQA, MedMCQA, MMLU).
    Comprehension & Retrieval: Evaluates the ability to read, synthesize, and extract answers from dense medical literature (e.g., PubMedQA, BioASQ).
    Clinical NLP & Information Extraction: Focuses on structured data extraction from unstructured text, such as NER, relation extraction, and de-identification (e.g., Medec, BC5CDR, i2b2).
    Documentation & Dialogue: Measures performance in ambient scribing, patient-facing chat, and summarization (e.g., ACI-Bench, MedDialog, MeQSum).
    Calculations & EHR/SQL: Tests structured data manipulation, medical math, and database querying (e.g., MedCalc, EHRSQL).
    Safety, Fairness & Hallucination: Critical for clinical deployment; measures bias mitigation, factual grounding, and refusal of harmful prompts (e.g., RaceBias, Med-Hallu, MedSafetyBench).
    Agentic & Workflow: Emerging benchmarks evaluating multi-step tool use, EHR navigation, and autonomous clinical workflows (e.g., MedAgentBench, HealthBench).
    Multimodal & Multilingual: Assesses cross-modal reasoning (text + imaging) and non-English clinical proficiency (e.g., GMAI-MMBench, JMedBench, CMExam).
